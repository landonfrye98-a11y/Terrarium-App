Seeds & blooms

Each task becomes a seed in the soil. Press ▶ while you work on it and a sprout grows; finish it and it blooms into a creature.
End a task with a day or time (“tomorrow 3pm”, “saturday”, “oct 24”) to give it a due date. Add steps after a colon (“Clean house sunday: trash, dishes, vacuum”), or paste a list. Tap a task to see its steps. Leave a task overdue and it grows weeds, and the longer you leave it, the more the glass suffers.
